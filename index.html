Academic Offering

Bachelor of Science in Nursing (B.Sc Nursing)

A rigorous 4-Year professional degree programme engineered to develop competent, empathetic nursing professionals ready for national and global healthcare systems.

Degree Type

4-Year Full-Time Professional Degree

Current Session

Academic Year 2026–2027

Core Clinical Curriculum Modules:

Foundations of Nursing: Basic patient care, medical ethics, CPR, and infection control protocols.
Medical-Surgical Nursing: Pre/post-operative management, adult patient care, and wound rehabilitation.
Child & Maternal Care: Neonatal care, pediatric therapy, and obstetrics & gynaecological nursing.
Community Health Nursing: Rural health outreach, family health assessment, and preventive medicine.

Internship & Rotations: Final year students complete extensive clinical rotations at Sree Saran Medical Center, obtaining documented practical hours in Intensive Care, OT, and Specialized Wards.

Admission Prerequisites

Admission Criteria & Quick Eligibility

Review the official entrance prerequisites for the B.Sc Nursing degree for the 2026–2027 academic session.

17–35 Years • 10+2 / HSC • PCB + English • Min 45% Aggregate • Medically Fit

Age Limits

  • • Minimum: 17 years as on 31st December of the admission year.
  • • Maximum: 35 years.

Education / Stream

  • • Passed 10+2 / Higher Secondary or equivalent examination.
  • • Science stream with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB).
  • • Must have passed with English Core / English Elective.

Marks & Recognized Boards

  • • Minimum 45% aggregate marks in qualifying science subjects.
  • • Boards accepted: AISSCE, CBSE, ICSE, SSCE, HSCE or other state recognized boards.

Medical Fitness & Status

  • • Candidate must be certified medically fit.
  • • Married candidates are also eligible to apply.
  • • Admission is conducted once a year.

Note for Applicants: Candidates who have completed their Higher Secondary Examination or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and English are eligible to proceed with provisional registration for the 2026–2027 batch.
